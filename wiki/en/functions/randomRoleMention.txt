<dash>
name: $randomRoleMention
desc: Returns a mention of a random role from the server.
category: Randomness

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: When $randomRoleMention is called, the bot collects all roles in the current server (excluding the default @everyone role), picks one at random, and sends its mention format to the channel.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $randomRoleMention
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: Congrats $randomRoleMention!
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The returned value is a Discord role mention (e.g., <@&123456789>), which renders as a clickable colored mention in the chat. The @everyone role is always excluded.
</block>
</dash>
