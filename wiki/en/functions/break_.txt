<dash>
name: $break
desc: Exits the nearest enclosing $for or $while loop immediately.
category: Flow Control

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: When $break is encountered inside a loop ($for or $while), the bot immediately stops iterating and continues execution after the matching $endfor or $endwhile. Any remaining iterations in the current loop are skipped.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $break
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $for[10]
  $if[$message == stop]
    $break
  $endif
$endfor
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: $break only exits the innermost loop it is currently in. If loops are nested, the outer loops continue executing normally.
</block>
</dash>
