<dash>
name: $endwhile
desc: Marks the end of a $while loop block.
category: Flow Control

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $endwhile signals the closing boundary of a $while loop. After each iteration, execution returns to the $while condition to re-evaluate it. Every $while must have a matching $endwhile.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $endwhile
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $setVar[count; 0]
$while[$getVar[count] < 5]
  $sendMessage[Count: $getVar[count]]
  $setVar[count; $math[$getVar[count] + 1]]
$endwhile
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: Every $while block must be properly closed with $endwhile. Missing $endwhile will cause a script error. Ensure your loop has a termination condition to avoid infinite loops.
</block>
</dash>
