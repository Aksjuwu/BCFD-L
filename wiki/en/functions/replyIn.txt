<dash>
name: $replyIn
desc: Delays the next reply by the bot for a specified duration.
category: Timing & Delays

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $replyIn pauses the script execution for the specified duration. After the delay, the script continues with the next command. This is useful for creating timed responses.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $replyIn[time]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $replyIn[5s]
$sendMessage[This appears after 5 seconds!]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The time format uses: s (seconds), m (minutes), h (hours), d (days). The script is frozen during the delay — no other commands in this script will execute until the delay completes.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $replyIn[time]
param: time|text|Required|Duration to wait. Format: number + unit (s/m/h/d), e.g., 5s, 10m, 1h
</details>
