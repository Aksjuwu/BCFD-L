<dash>
name: $editChannelPerms
desc: Edits permission overwrites for a role or member on a channel.
category: Moderation

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $editChannelPerms modifies the permission overwrites for a specific role or member on a channel. Use +perm to allow, -perm to deny, or /perm to reset a permission. The bot must have "Manage Channels" permission.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $editChannelPerms[channel ID; target ID; perm1; perm2; ...]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $editChannelPerms[123456789; 987654321; +send_messages; -connect]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: The bot must have "Manage Channels" or "Manage Permissions" permission. Target can be a role or member ID/mention.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Permission names use Discord's snake_case format: send_messages, view_channel, connect, manage_roles, etc. Use + to allow, - to deny, / or ~ to reset.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $editChannelPerms[channel ID; target ID; perm1; perm2; ...]
param: channelID|text|Required|The channel ID or mention to edit permissions on.
param: targetID|text|Required|The role or member ID/mention to set permissions for.
param: permissions|text|Required|One or more permissions with +/- prefix (e.g., +send_messages, -connect).
</details>
