<dash>
name: $uptime
desc: Returns how long the bot has been online since its last startup.
category: Utilities

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: When $uptime is called, the bot calculates the elapsed time since its last startup (on_ready event) and formats it into a human-readable string, then sends it to the channel.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $uptime
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: Bot has been up for $uptime
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The formatted output includes days, hours, minutes, and seconds as applicable (e.g., 2d 5h 30m 12s). The bot must have called set_bot_start_time() during startup for this to work.
</block>
</dash>
