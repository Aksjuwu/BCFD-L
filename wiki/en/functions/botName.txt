<dash>
name: $botName
desc: Returns the username of the bot.
category: Info

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: When $botName is called, the bot retrieves its own username and sends it directly to the channel as a plain text string.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $botName
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: I am $botName!
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: This returns the bot's current username. If the bot's username is changed via the Developer Portal, the next call will reflect the updated name.
</block>
</dash>
