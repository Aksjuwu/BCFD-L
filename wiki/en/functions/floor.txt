<dash>
name: $floor
desc: Rounds a number down to the nearest integer.
category: Math & Numbers

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $floor takes a single numeric value and rounds it down to the nearest whole number (floor). For example, 3.9 becomes 3, and -2.1 becomes -3. Works both as a standalone command and inline inside other commands.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $floor[number]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $sendMessage[Result is $floor[3.9]]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: $floor[3.9] returns 3. $floor[-2.1] returns -3. $floor[5.0] returns 5. The result is always an integer.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $floor[number]
param: number|number|Required|The number to round down to the nearest integer, e.g., 3.9, -2.1, 10
</details>
