<dash>
name: $slowmode
desc: Sets the slowmode delay for a specified channel.
category: Moderation

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $slowmode takes a channel ID and a time duration, then sets the slowmode delay for that channel. Slowmode limits how often users can send messages.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $slowmode[channelID; time]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $slowmode[$channelID; 10s]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The time format uses: s (seconds), m (minutes), h (hours). Maximum slowmode is 6 hours (21600 seconds). Use 0s to disable slowmode.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: The bot must have "Manage Channels" permission in the target channel.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $slowmode[channelID; time]
param: channelID|snowflake|Required|The ID of the channel to set slowmode for
param: time|text|Required|Duration of the slowmode. Format: number + unit (s/m/h), e.g., 10s, 5m, 0s to disable
</details>
