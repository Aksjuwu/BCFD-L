<dash>
name: $mod
desc: Returns the remainder of dividing the first number by each subsequent number.
category: Math & Numbers

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $mod takes two or more numeric arguments. It computes the modulo (remainder) of the first value divided by each subsequent value sequentially. Works both as a standalone command and inline.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $mod[a; b; ...]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $sendMessage[$mod[10; 3]]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: Modulo by zero returns an error. $mod[10; 3] evaluates to 1 (the remainder of 10 / 3).
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $mod[a; b; ...]
param: a|number|Required|The dividend (number to divide)
param: b|number|Required|The divisor (cannot be 0). Additional divisors are applied sequentially.
</details>
