<dash>
name: $getCreationDateTimestamp
desc: Converts any ID into its creation date as a Unix timestamp.
category: Timing & Delays

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: Every Discord object (user, channel, role, message...) contains its creation time inside its snowflake ID. $getCreationDateTimestamp decodes it and returns the creation moment as a Unix timestamp (seconds since January 1, 1970). Mentions are accepted and cleaned automatically.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $getCreationDateTimestamp[idOrMention]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $sendMessage[Your account was created on <t:$getCreationDateTimestamp[$authorID]>]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Works with any snowflake ID: users, members, channels, roles, servers and messages. Combine with Discord's <t:TIMESTAMP> format to display a readable date.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $getCreationDateTimestamp[idOrMention]
param: idOrMention|text|Required|A snowflake ID or mention (<@id>, <#id>, <@&id>) of any Discord object
</details>
