<dash>
name: $footer
desc: Sets the footer text of the embed being built.
category: Embed Builder

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $footer takes a text argument and sets it as the footer of the current embed being constructed. The footer appears at the very bottom of the embed in smaller, lighter text.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $footer[text]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $footer[Requested by $authorName]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The footer is useful for attribution, timestamps, or additional context. It appears in smaller grey text at the bottom of the embed.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $footer[text]
param: text|text|Required|The footer text for the embed
</details>
