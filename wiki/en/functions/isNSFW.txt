<dash>
name: $isNSFW
desc: Checks if a channel is marked as NSFW.
category: Channel

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $isNSFW takes a channel mention or ID and checks whether the channel is marked as NSFW (Not Safe For Work). Returns "true" if the channel is NSFW, "false" otherwise.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $isNSFW[channel mention/ID]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $isNSFW[<#123456789012345678>]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Useful for conditional logic to restrict content based on channel age-rating.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $isNSFW[channel mention/ID]
param: channel|text|Required|The channel mention or numeric ID to check.
</details>
