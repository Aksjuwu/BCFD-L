<dash>
name: $sendEmbedMessage
desc: Sends an embed message to a specified channel with title, description, color, and footer.
category: Messaging

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $sendEmbedMessage constructs an embed with the provided title, description, color, and footer, then sends it to the specified channel. All five arguments are required.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $sendEmbedMessage[channelID; title; description; color; footer]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $sendEmbedMessage[$channelID; Welcome!; Thanks for joining; #00FF00; Enjoy your stay]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The color argument accepts hex values (e.g., #FF5733). The bot must have "Send Messages" and "Embed Links" permissions in the target channel.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $sendEmbedMessage[channelID; title; description; color; footer]
param: channelID|snowflake|Required|The ID of the channel to send the embed to
param: title|text|Required|The title of the embed
param: description|text|Required|The description/body text of the embed
param: color|text|Required|Hex color code for the embed accent bar (e.g., #FF5733)
param: footer|text|Required|Footer text displayed at the bottom of the embed
</details>
