<dash>
name: $channelName
desc: Returns the name of the channel where the command was used.
category: Info

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: When $channelName is called, the bot retrieves the name of the current channel and sends it directly to the channel as a plain text string.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $channelName
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: You are chatting in #$channelName
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: This returns the channel's current name without the # prefix. If the channel is renamed, the next call will reflect the updated name.
</block>
</dash>
