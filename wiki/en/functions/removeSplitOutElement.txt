<dash>
name: $removeSplitOutElement
desc: Removes one element from the current split result ($splitIn) by its 1-based index.
category: String Utilities

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $removeSplitOutElement deletes the element at the given position from the data stored by $splitIn. The remaining elements keep their order. $splitIn must be called before this command.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $removeSplitOutElement[index]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $splitIn[apple,banana,cherry; ,]
$removeSplitOutElement[2]
$joinSplitOut[; ]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The index must be a positive integer within the current split length, otherwise an error is raised.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $removeSplitOutElement[index]
param: index|number|Required|1-based position of the element to remove
</details>
