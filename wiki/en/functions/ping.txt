<dash>
name: $ping
desc: Returns the bot's latency (response time) to the Discord gateway in milliseconds.
category: Utilities

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: The command fetches the current connection speed between the bot and the Discord Gateway, then sends the result directly as a raw number representing the time in milliseconds.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $ping
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $ping pong!
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The resulting value represents the responsiveness of the bot's server. The lower the returned number, the faster the bot will respond to commands and interactions.
</block>
</dash>
