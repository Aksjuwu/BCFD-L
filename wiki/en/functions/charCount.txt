<dash>
name: $charCount
desc: Returns the number of alphanumeric characters in the given text.
category: Math & Numbers

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $charCount takes a text argument and counts only alphanumeric characters (letters and digits), ignoring spaces, symbols, and punctuation. It returns the count as a number.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $charCount[text]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $charCount[Hello World!]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Only letters (from any language) and digits (0-9) are counted. Spaces, punctuation, and special characters are excluded from the count.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $charCount[text]
param: text|text|Required|The text to count alphanumeric characters in. Variables and placeholders are resolved before counting.
</details>
