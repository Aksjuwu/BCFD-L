<dash>
name: $mul
desc: Multiplies two or more numbers together and returns the result.
category: Math & Numbers

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $mul takes two or more numeric arguments and returns their product. Works both as a standalone command and inline inside other commands.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $mul[a; b; ...]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $sendMessage[$mul[3; 4]]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Supports any number of arguments. $mul[2; 3; 4] evaluates to 24. The result is an integer if the product has no decimal part.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $mul[a; b; ...]
param: a|number|Required|First number to multiply
param: b|number|Required|Second number to multiply (additional numbers are also accepted)
</details>
