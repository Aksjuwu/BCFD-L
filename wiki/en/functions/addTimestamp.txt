<dash>
name: $addTimestamp
desc: Fetches the current time and sends it as a dynamic Discord timestamp format.
category: Timing & Delays

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: When $addTimestamp is invoked, the bot calculates the current time, converts it into Discord's dynamic timestamp format (Long Time format), and sends the result directly to the destination channel.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $addTimestamp
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $addTimestamp
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The sent timestamp uses the Long Time format, meaning it will automatically adjust and display to every Discord user according to their device's local time zone (e.g., 6:46:58 PM).
</block>
</dash>
