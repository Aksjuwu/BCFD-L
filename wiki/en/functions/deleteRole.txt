<dash>
name: $deleteRole
desc: Deletes a role from the server.
category: Moderation

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $deleteRole takes a role ID or @mention, looks it up in the server, and permanently deletes it. This action cannot be undone.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $deleteRole[role ID or @mention]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $deleteRole[&123456789]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: The bot must have "Manage Roles" permission and its highest role must be above the target role. Deleting a role is permanent and cannot be undone.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $deleteRole[role ID or @mention]
param: role ID or @mention|text|Required|The ID or @mention of the role to delete
</details>
