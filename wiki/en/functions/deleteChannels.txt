<dash>
name: $deleteChannels
desc: Deletes one or more channels from the server.
category: Channel Management

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $deleteChannels takes one or more channel IDs or mentions and deletes them from the server. The bot must have "Manage Channels" permission. If any channel cannot be deleted (e.g., missing permissions), the operation stops with an error.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $deleteChannels[channel ID/mention; ...]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $deleteChannels[<#123456789012345678>]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: This command permanently deletes channels. The bot must have "Manage Channels" permission for each channel being deleted.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $deleteChannels[channel ID/mention; ...]
param: channels|text|Required|One or more channel IDs or mentions separated by semicolons.
</details>
