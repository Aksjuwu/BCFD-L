<dash>
name: $setBotStatus
desc: Changes the bot's presence status (online, idle, dnd or invisible).
category: Server

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $setBotStatus switches the bot's presence indicator between online, idle, dnd (do not disturb) and invisible (appears offline). The status stays until changed again - restarting the app resets it according to the saved settings.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $setBotStatus[online|idle|dnd|invisible]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $setBotStatus[idle]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="important">
text: Rate-limited internally to one change every 12 seconds - extra calls within that window fail silently. offline is accepted as an alias for invisible. Anything else raises an error.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $setBotStatus[online|idle|dnd|invisible]
param: status|text|Required|One of: online, idle, dnd, invisible (offline works as alias for invisible)
</details>
