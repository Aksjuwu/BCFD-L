<dash>
name: $returnGuildUsersID
desc: Collects human (non-bot) member IDs from a guild and stores them.
category: Return Commands

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $returnGuildUsersID collects all human (non-bot) member IDs from a specified guild and stores them under a variable name. You can choose between "cache" mode (uses already cached members) or "chunk" mode (forces a full guild member fetch first for complete data). The result can be retrieved later using $return[variableName].
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $returnGuildUsersID[guildID; cache/chunk; var; separator]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $returnGuildUsersID[$guildID; cache; userIDs; ,]
$sendMessage[Users: $return[userIDs]]
</block>

<block type="options" title="Fetch modes">
option: cache|Uses the bot's cached member list (fast, but may be incomplete for large servers)
option: chunk|Forces a full member list fetch from Discord first (slower, but complete)
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Only human (non-bot) members are included. The bot must be a member of the target guild. Use "chunk" mode for large servers where the cached member list may be incomplete.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $returnGuildUsersID[guildID; cache/chunk; var; separator]
param: guildID|snowflake|Required|The ID of the guild (server) to collect member IDs from
param: cache/chunk|text|Required|Fetch mode: "cache" for cached members (fast), "chunk" for full fetch (complete)
param: var|text|Required|The variable name to store the result in (use with $return[])
param: separator|text|Required|The separator between multiple IDs (e.g., , or ;). You can use named separators: dot, com, apo, sem, colon
</details>
