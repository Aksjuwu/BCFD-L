<dash>
name: $wait
desc: Pauses the script execution for a specified duration.
category: Timing & Delays

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $wait freezes the entire script execution for the specified duration. No subsequent commands in the script will execute until the wait period has elapsed.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $wait[time]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $sendMessage[Starting...]
$wait[5s]
$sendMessage[Done after 5 seconds!]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The time format uses: s (seconds), m (minutes), h (hours), d (days). The entire script is frozen during the wait — other scripts from other users are not affected.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $wait[time]
param: time|text|Required|Duration to wait. Format: number + unit (s/m/h/d), e.g., 5s, 10m, 1h, 1d
</details>
