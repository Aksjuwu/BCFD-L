<dash>
name: $div
desc: Divides the first number by each subsequent number and returns the result.
category: Math & Numbers

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $div takes two or more numeric arguments. It starts with the first value and divides it by each subsequent value. Works both as a standalone command and inline.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $div[a; b; ...]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $sendMessage[$div[10; 2]]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: Division by zero returns an error. $div[10; 2; 5] evaluates to 10 / 2 / 5 = 1. The result is an integer if the quotient has no decimal part.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $div[a; b; ...]
param: a|number|Required|The dividend (number to divide)
param: b|number|Required|The divisor (cannot be 0). Additional divisors are applied sequentially.
</details>
