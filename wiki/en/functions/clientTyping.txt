<dash>
name: $clientTyping
desc: Shows the bot's "typing..." indicator in the current channel.
category: Utilities

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: When $clientTyping is called, the bot displays the "Bot is typing..." indicator in the current channel, signaling to users that the bot is processing or preparing a response. This indicator persists until the bot sends a message or the script ends.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $clientTyping
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $clientTyping
$wait[3s]
$sendMessage[Done!]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: This command is useful for simulating a more natural conversation flow. Combine it with $wait to create a deliberate delay before the bot sends its next message.
</block>
</dash>
