<dash>
name: $getBotInvent
desc: Generates and sends the bot's OAuth2 invite link.
category: Utilities

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: When $getBotInvent is called, the bot constructs an OAuth2 authorization URL using its application ID with full permissions (8) and the bot scope, then sends the link to the channel.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $getBotInvent
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: Invite me: $getBotInvent
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The generated link includes all permissions (permissions=8). The link format is: https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id={id}&permissions=8&scope=bot
</block>
</dash>
