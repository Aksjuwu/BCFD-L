<dash>
name: $endif
desc: Marks the end of an $if or $elif block.
category: Flow Control

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $endif signals the closing boundary of a conditional $if block. All code between $if and $endif is only executed when the condition is met. Every $if must have a matching $endif.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $endif
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $if[$authorID == 123456789]
  Hello Admin!
$endif
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: Every $if block must be properly closed with $endif. Missing $endif will cause a script error.
</block>
</dash>
