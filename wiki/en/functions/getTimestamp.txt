<dash>
name: $getTimestamp
desc: Returns the current time as a Unix timestamp.
category: Timing & Delays

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $getTimestamp returns the number of seconds elapsed since January 1, 1970 (UTC) as plain digits - the classic Unix epoch time. It is refreshed every time the script runs, so it always reflects the exact moment of execution.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $getTimestamp
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $sendMessage[Now in Unix time: $getTimestamp]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Combine it with <t:$getTimestamp> in your message text to let Discord render a live local date and time for every reader.
</block>
</dash>
