<dash>
name: $randomint
desc: Returns a random integer between a minimum and maximum value.
category: Randomness

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $randomint takes two numbers (min and max) and generates a random integer within that inclusive range, then sends it to the channel.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $randomint[min; max]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $randomint[1; 100]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Both min and max are inclusive. If min is greater than max, the values are automatically swapped. The result is a whole number between the two bounds.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $randomint[min; max]
param: min|number|Required|The minimum value (inclusive)
param: max|number|Required|The maximum value (inclusive)
</details>
