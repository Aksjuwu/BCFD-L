<dash>
name: $title
desc: Sets the title of the embed being built.
category: Embed Builder

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $title takes a text argument and sets it as the title of the current embed being constructed. The title appears at the top of the embed in larger, bold text.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $title[text]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $title[Server Rules]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The title supports Discord markdown formatting. It appears as the main heading at the top of the embed.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $title[text]
param: text|text|Required|The title text for the embed
</details>
