<dash>
name: $replaceText
desc: Replaces occurrences of a substring within a text with new text.
category: String Utilities

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $replaceText takes a message, an old text to find, and a new text to replace it with. Optionally, you can specify how many occurrences to replace. Returns the modified text.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $replaceText[message; old; new; (amount)]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $replaceText[Hello World; World; FDScript]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: By default, all occurrences are replaced. Provide a number as the 4th argument to limit replacements. Use -1 or omit to replace all.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $replaceText[message; old; new; (amount)]
param: message|text|Required|The original text to search within
param: old|text|Required|The substring to find and replace
param: new|text|Required|The replacement text
param: amount|number|Optional|Maximum number of replacements. -1 or omitted = replace all occurrences
</details>
