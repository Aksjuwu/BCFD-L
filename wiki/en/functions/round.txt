<dash>
name: $round
desc: Rounds a number to the nearest whole number.
category: Math & Numbers

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $round takes a single numeric value, rounds it to the nearest integer, and sends the result. For example, 1.7 becomes 2 and 1.4 becomes 1.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $round[number]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $round[1.7]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: $round[1.7] returns 2. $round[2.4] returns 2. The command accepts a single argument; if the value is not a valid number, an error is returned.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $round[number]
param: number|number|Required|The number to round to the nearest whole number
</details>
