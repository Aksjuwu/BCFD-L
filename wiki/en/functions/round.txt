<dash>
name: $round
desc: Rounds a number to a specified number of decimal places.
category: Math & Numbers

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $round takes a number and optionally a precision (number of decimal places), then rounds it accordingly and sends the result. If no precision is given, it rounds to the nearest integer.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $round[number; (precision)]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $round[3.14159; 2]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Without the precision argument, the number is rounded to the nearest whole number. With precision, it rounds to that many decimal places.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $round[number; (precision)]
param: number|number|Required|The number to round
param: precision|number|Optional|Number of decimal places to round to. If omitted, rounds to the nearest integer.
</details>
