<dash>
name: $createRole
desc: Creates a new role in the server with specified name, color, and permissions.
category: Moderation

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $createRole creates a new role with the given name, color, and permissions. Permissions can be specified as a preset (cosmetic, member, mod, manager) or a raw permission number. Optionally, you can control whether the role is displayed separately and whether it is mentionable.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $createRole[role name; color; permissions; (display role; mention role)]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $createRole[VIP; #FFD700; member; yes; no]
</block>

<block type="options" title="Permission presets">
option: cosmetic|No special permissions (display-only role)
option: member|Basic member permissions (send messages, react, connect, etc.)
option: mod|Moderation permissions (kick, ban, manage messages, timeout, etc.)
option: manager|Full management permissions (manage channels, roles, webhooks, etc.)
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The created role's ID is stored in $return[createdRoleID] for later use. The bot must have "Manage Roles" permission.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $createRole[role name; color; permissions; (display role; mention role)]
param: role name|text|Required|The name for the new role (max 100 characters)
param: color|text|Required|Hex color code (e.g., #FF5733) or empty for default
param: permissions|text|Required|A preset (cosmetic, member, mod, manager) or a raw permission integer
param: display role|yes/no|Optional|yes to display the role separately in the member list (default: no)
param: mention role|yes/no|Optional|yes to make the role mentionable by everyone (default: no)
</details>
