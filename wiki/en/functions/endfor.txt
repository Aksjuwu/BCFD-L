<dash>
name: $endfor
desc: Marks the end of a $for loop block.
category: Flow Control

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $endfor signals the closing boundary of a $for loop. After each iteration, execution returns to the top of the loop body. Every $for must have a matching $endfor.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $endfor
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $for[5]
  $sendMessage[Iteration!]
$endfor
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: Every $for block must be properly closed with $endfor. Missing $endfor will cause a script error.
</block>
</dash>
