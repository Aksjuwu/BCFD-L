<dash>
name: $authorName
desc: Returns the username of the person who triggered the command.
category: Info

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: When $authorName is called, the bot retrieves the username of the command author and sends it directly to the channel as a plain text string.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $authorName
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: Hello, $authorName!
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: This returns the user's current username (not their display/nickname). If the user changes their username, the next call will reflect the updated name.
</block>
</dash>
