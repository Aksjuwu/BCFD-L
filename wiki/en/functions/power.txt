<dash>
name: $power
desc: Raises a number to the power of another number.
category: Math & Numbers

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $power takes two numeric arguments — a base and an exponent — and raises the base to the power of the exponent. Works both as a standalone command and inline inside other commands.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $power[base; exponent]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $sendMessage[$power[2; 10]]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: $power[2; 10] returns 1024. $power[3; 3] returns 27. $power[5; 0] returns 1. The result is an integer if the result has no decimal part.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: Negative base with a fractional exponent results in a complex number, which returns an error. Very large results may also trigger an overflow error.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $power[base; exponent]
param: base|number|Required|The base number to raise, e.g., 2, 10, 3.5
param: exponent|number|Required|The exponent (power), e.g., 3, 10, 0.5
</details>
